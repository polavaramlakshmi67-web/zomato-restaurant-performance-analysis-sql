# Zomato Restaurant Performance Analysis Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato restaurant data using SQL to identify trends in restaurant performance, customer preferences, cuisine popularity, ratings, online delivery services, and table booking facilities.

The objective is to generate business insights that can support restaurant aggregators and business stakeholders in making data-driven decisions.

---

## Dataset Information

Dataset: Zomato Restaurant Dataset

Attributes Used:

- Restaurant Name
- City
- Cuisine
- Aggregate Rating
- Average Cost for Two
- Online Delivery
- Table Booking
- Votes

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze restaurant distribution across cities
- Identify top-performing restaurants
- Discover popular cuisines
- Evaluate the impact of online delivery
- Evaluate the impact of table booking
- Compare restaurant pricing across cities
- Rank restaurants within each city

## Key Insights

1. Restaurant distribution varies significantly across cities.
2. Certain cuisines dominate the restaurant market.
3. Online delivery influences restaurant ratings and customer reach.
4. Restaurants offering table booking generally demonstrate stronger customer engagement.
5. Premium restaurants tend to maintain higher average ratings.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Expand restaurant partnerships in high-performing cities.
2. Promote top-rated cuisines through targeted campaigns.
3. Encourage adoption of online delivery services.
4. Improve visibility of highly rated restaurants.
5. Focus customer experience initiatives on lower-rated restaurants.
